DID THE BREADTH FIX DELIVER WHAT THE POWER AUDIT PREDICTED?
==============================================================================
  predicted annual Sharpe at IC=0.03, 300 names, haircut 0.25     0.90
  measured annual Sharpe, L5 gross                                  0.36
  -> implied IC at the assumed 0.25 haircut                       0.0122
  -> implied haircut at the assumed IC of 0.03                     0.041

  The Fundamental Law counts INDEPENDENT bets. Thirty longs and thirty shorts chosen by
  one signal are not sixty bets, they are one factor bet held sixty ways, and the monthly
  series is what the design actually gets to average over. The 0.25 haircut in power.py
  is far too generous for a cross-sectional factor; measured here it is nearer 0.04.

  Consequence for the audit: `results/crsp_audit.txt` reports 96.5% power for 300 names,
  which is power against a Sharpe of 0.90 that this universe does not produce. Power against
  the LITERATURE effect of 0.40 is what the family reports above, and it is far lower.
  Minimum detectable annual Sharpe at |t|>2.64 over 24.5 years: 0.53.

  DSR here deflates against the expected best of 6 trials using a Sharpe variance of 0.012587, estimated from the 2 families this screen ran.
  That estimate is the weakest input in the procedure and it is stated rather than buried: with two trials it is barely an estimate.
  It is also not the primary correction. Nothing was SELECTED here -- both hypotheses were
  registered before the data existed -- so Bonferroni across the family is the instrument that
  applies, and DSR is reported as a cross-check, not as the verdict.
